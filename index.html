How often does “live” mean?

Read before use

The page requests official feeds at load and every five minutes. A five-minute refresh does not make the original publisher release data more often.

RBI publishes the USD/INR reference rate around 1 p.m. on business days and labels equity/bond data with their own “as on” dates. CPI is released monthly; GDP is released quarterly. Those figures can be current official data while still being older than today.

TradingView embeds market charts, but their exchange data may be delayed unless the viewer has the required market-data entitlement. This page does not claim tick-by-tick NSE access.

If a source is unavailable or its value cannot be read, the page shows “Unavailable” instead of reusing an old example.
